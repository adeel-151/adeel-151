## About

I'm a **React Frontend Specialist** with full-stack **MERN** capability. I build clean, fast, accessible web applications, from polished interfaces to the APIs and databases behind them.

- Recently shipped a production-ready **School Management ERP** (five user roles, full RBAC) and architected a Pakistan-focused **multi-vendor marketplace**
- Trained at **Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT)**, and now an **academy instructor** teaching Digital Marketing and Social Media Marketing
- Currently exploring **Next.js**, **GraphQL**, and **AWS**
- Ask me about **React**, **Node.js**, **TypeScript**, or **system design**
- Open to **freelance work, collaborations, and mentoring**
